# openCalory Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers of **openCalory** pledge to make participation in our project and community a welcoming, inclusive, harassment-free, and respectful experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, race, or religion.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respectful discussions of differing viewpoints and architectural decisions
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community, self-hosters, and users

Examples of unacceptable behavior:
- Harassment, derogatory comments, or personal attacks
- Trolling, insulting, or disrespectful communication
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Any conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement & Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

To report any concerns or violations, please contact the repository maintainers via GitHub or the email listed in their profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept confidential.
